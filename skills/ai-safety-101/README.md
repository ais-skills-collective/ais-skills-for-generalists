# AI Safety 101

A Claude skill for understanding the AI safety field — concepts, motivations, structure, contested questions, and where to start.

## Who it's for

Anyone coming to AI safety for the first time, or from an adjacent background (policy, product, ML, journalism, curious generalist). The skill builds genuine intuition rather than vocabulary mimicry.

## What it covers

- Core concepts explained from first principles (RLHF, deceptive alignment, interpretability, reward hacking, and ~30 more)
- Why the field exists and why specific problems are hard
- How the subfields fit together (alignment, governance, evals, interpretability)
- Contested questions and where serious people disagree
- Where to start reading

## What it doesn't do

Deep upskilling (courses, fellowships, structured learning plans) — that's the [AI Safety Learning Paths](../ai-safety-learning-paths/) skill.
