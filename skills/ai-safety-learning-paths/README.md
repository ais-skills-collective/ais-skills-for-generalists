# AI Safety Learning Paths

A Claude skill for building a personalised AI safety learning plan based on your background, target track, and time available.

## Who it's for

Generalists and career-changers who want to develop real competence in AI safety — not just understand the field, but build skills and credentials to contribute to it. Covers all career tracks.

## What it covers

- Personalised learning plans sequenced across three horizons (now / soon / later)
- Program and fellowship comparisons (BlueDot vs ARENA, MATS vs LASR, GovAI vs TALOS, and more)
- Track-specific entry points across technical research, governance, communications, field-building, ops, evals, AI security, legal/regulatory, and biosecurity
- Current deadlines and cohort status via web search
- Background-to-track matching for 10+ professional backgrounds
- Full-time vs part-time filtering

## What it doesn't do

Field orientation and conceptual grounding — that's the [AI Safety 101](../ai-safety-101/) skill. Career path matching and role archetypes — that's the [AI Safety Career Paths](../ai-safety-career-paths/) skill.
