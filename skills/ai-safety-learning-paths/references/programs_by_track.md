## 1. Foundational courses (all tracks)

These are appropriate starting points regardless of track. Most are part-time and
async-compatible.

| Program | Format | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **BlueDot Impact — AI Safety Fundamentals (Alignment)** | Online cohort, part-time | Core conceptual grounding; runs multiple cohorts per year |
| **BlueDot Impact — AI Safety Fundamentals (Governance)** | Online cohort, part-time | Policy-oriented version of the above; good for non-technical entrants |
| **AGI Safety Fundamentals** | Online cohort, part-time | Now run under BlueDot Impact; original AGISF curriculum |
| **BlueDot Impact — AI Security course** | Online, part-time | Newer track focused on AI security fundamentals |
| **AISES — AI Safety, Ethics and Society** | Free online textbook + virtual course | Comprehensive introductory course by Dan Hendrycks (Center for AI Safety). Covers risks, governance, and ethics. Good standalone read or pre-reading before a BlueDot cohort. See: https://www.aisafetybook.com |
| **ARENA** | Online + in-person options, intensive | Hands-on technical alignment curriculum; best for those wanting interpretability or alignment engineering. See: https://www.arena.education |
| **AI Safety Colab** | Online cohort, part-time | 8-week intro program run by ENAIS; two tracks (governance follows BlueDot; alignment follows AI Safety Atlas). Good entry point for newcomers regardless of background |
| **AISafety.com** | Aggregator | Not a program — a directory of courses, fellowships, and entry points. Search here for current options: https://aisafety.com |

## 2. Technical alignment research

High-bar programs. Most require prior ML knowledge or quantitative research experience.
Full-time unless noted.

| Program | Format | Prestige / notes |
|---|---|---|
| **MATS (ML Alignment Theory Scholars)** | Full-time, in-person Berkeley | Highest-prestige technical fellowship currently. Pairs scholars with senior mentors (e.g. Anthropic, Redwood, ARC). Competitive. See: https://www.matsprogram.org |
| **Anthropic Fellows Program** | Full-time, 4-month research fellowship | Embedded at Anthropic; covers interpretability, robustness, AI control, model welfare. Doesn't require PhD or published papers. See: https://alignment.anthropic.com |
| **OpenAI Safety Fellowship** | Full-time, ~5 months | External researchers; empirical safety focus. See: https://openai.com |
| **LASR Labs** | Full-time, in-person London, 13 weeks | London-based; teams of 3-4 write academic paper. Strong alumni placement (UK AISI, Apollo, OpenAI evals). See: https://www.lasrlabs.org |
| **ERA Fellowship (Cambridge ERA:AI)** | Full-time, in-person Cambridge, 10 weeks | Technical safety or governance. Stipend + accommodation. See: https://erafellowship.org |
| **PIBBSS Fellowship** | Full-time, ~3 months, London/remote | For PhD/postdoc researchers from complex systems sciences transitioning to AIS. Run by Principles of Intelligence (formerly PIBBSS / pibbss.ai — pibbss.ai now redirects here). See: https://princint.ai/programs/fellowship |
| **PIBBSS Research Residency** | Full-time, 6 weeks, London (LISA) | Applied mathematics residency run with Iliad; PhD/postdoc level. Run by Principles of Intelligence (formerly PIBBSS). See: https://princint.ai/programs/residency |
| **Global AI Safety Fellowship** | Full-time, placement-based | High bar — requires first-author ML paper or equivalent. See: https://globalaisafetyfellowship.com |
| **Algoverse AI Safety Research Fellowship** | Full-time, 12 weeks | Open Philanthropy-funded; 25+ hrs/week; team paper output. See: https://algoverseairesearch.org |
| **SPAR (Student and Professional AI Risk Program)** | Part-time, remote, 3 months | Flexible 5-40 hrs/week; pairs with professional mentors; technical or policy tracks. Good for people who can't go full-time. See: https://sparai.org |
| **FLI PhD Fellowships** | PhD-track | Future of Life Institute fellowship for PhD researchers working on AIS. See: https://futureoflife.org/grant-program/phd-fellowships |
| **Constellation — Astra Fellowship** | Full-time, in-person Berkeley, 3-6 months | Pairs emerging researchers with senior advisors; technical safety, governance, strategy, or field-building. See: https://constellation.org/programs |
| **Constellation — Visiting Researcher Program** | Full-time, in-person Berkeley, 3-6 months | Lightly-structured; for established researchers wanting community and workspace. See: https://constellation.org/programs |

| Program | Format | Notes |
|---|---|---|

| **IAPS AI Policy Fellowship** | Varies | Institute for AI Policy and Strategy; US-focused |

| **80,000 Hours career advising** | Free, 1-1 calls | Not a program but a high-value entry point; prioritises people with strong track-matching. See: https://80000hours.org/speak-with-us |
| **ERA Fellowship** | Full-time, Cambridge | Covers governance track as well as technical. See Track 2 entry above |
| **SPAR** | Part-time, remote | Policy track available; see Track 2 entry above |

## 6. Evaluations, red-teaming, and auditing

One of the fastest-growing hiring areas. Most roles are at labs or AIS orgs; fellowships
are the primary entry pathway.

| Program | Format | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **MATS** | Full-time, Berkeley | Several streams focus on evals and dangerous capabilities. See Track 2 |
| **Anthropic Fellows Program** | Full-time | Covers evaluations and adversarial robustness tracks. See Track 2 |
| **LASR Labs** | Full-time, London | Strong evals-to-placement pipeline; many alumni at OpenAI dangerous capabilities evals team, Apollo. See Track 2 |
| **ERA Fellowship** | Full-time, Cambridge | Research can be directed toward evals. See Track 2 |
| **METR** | Direct application | Key evals org; worth monitoring their job board directly. See: https://metr.org |
| **Apollo Research** | Direct application | Frontier AI safety and evals. See: https://apolloresearch.ai |
| **UK AI Security Institute (AISI)** | Direct application | Government evals body; also hires from fellowship alumni pipelines |
