# Background to Track — Quick Reference

> **How to use this file**
> Use this as a fast heuristic when a user's background is clear but their target
> track is not. These are starting hypotheses, not fixed rules — always check with
> the user before committing to a track recommendation.
>
> For deeper background-to-track matching, refer to the `ai-safety-career-paths`
> skill, which carries a more detailed role archetypes model and reasoning about
> which backgrounds map to which AIS entry points.

---

| Background | Likely strong tracks | Key transferable assets | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Software engineer / ML engineer** | Technical alignment, evaluations/red-teaming, AI security | Ability to implement; systems thinking | May need to build research taste and AIS conceptual grounding before fellowships |
| **Academic researcher (STEM)** | Technical alignment, evaluations | Research methodology; ability to produce outputs | Field may feel different from academia — worth doing a conceptual course first |
| **Academic researcher (social sciences / humanities)** | Governance, policy, comms/journalism | Analytical writing; institutional knowledge | May need to bridge to AIS-specific framing; PIBBSS or Pivotal are good fits |
| **Policy / civil servant** | Governance and policy, legal/regulatory | Understanding of how rules get made; stakeholder navigation | AIS-specific knowledge gap; BlueDot governance track is a good fast foundation |
| **Lawyer / legal professional** | Legal/regulatory, governance | Regulatory analysis; precision in language | LawAI fellowship is the clearest direct entry point |
| **Journalist / communicator** | Communications, field-building | Public audience reach; ability to translate complexity | Tarbell is the primary fellowship; Frame for broader comms roles |
| **Biology / public health / life sciences** | Biosecurity/GCR, governance | Dual-use risk intuitions; institutional review familiarity; interdisciplinary framing | Pivotal and PIBBSS are the strongest entry points |
| **Philosophy / ethics** | Technical alignment (conceptual), governance, policy | Conceptual rigour; comfort with uncertainty | May need to build either technical depth or policy writing credentials |
| **Ops / programme management / nonprofit** | Field-building, ops, generalist | Project delivery; stakeholder management; systems navigation | Generator Residency at Constellation is a direct match; BlueDot first |
| **Business / finance** | Ops, field-building, governance | Organisational and resource thinking | Less direct path; field-building or operations roles most accessible |
| **Education / facilitation** | Field-building, communications | Community engagement; curriculum design | AIS Colab and BlueDot as foundations; Generator Residency worth exploring |
