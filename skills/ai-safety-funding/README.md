# AI Safety Funding

A Claude skill for navigating AI safety funding — finding the right funders, assessing fit and understanding application processes.

## Who it's for

Early-stage founders, independent researchers, existing orgs raising follow-on, and pivoters seeking project funding. Also useful for anyone exploring whether their idea is fundable at all.

## What it covers

- Prioritised funder shortlists matched to your project shape, stage, and ask size
- Honest fit verdicts, not just a dumped list
- The full funding landscape: speculative grants, strategic grantmakers, career-transition funds, incubators, safety-aligned VC, government programmes, prizes, and RFPs
- Application process logistics and how to handle deadlines (via web search for live status)
- Light pitch and proposal review against signals funders actually look for
- When and how to route to a human advisor or review service

## What it doesn't do

Career direction and pivot decisions — that's the [AI Safety Career Paths](../ai-safety-career-paths/) skill. Learning plans and fellowship recommendations — that's the [AI Safety Learning Paths](../ai-safety-learning-paths/) skill.
