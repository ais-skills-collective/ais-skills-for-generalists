# AI Safety Career Paths

A Claude skill for mapping your existing background onto realistic AI safety career options.

## Who it's for

Mid-career and senior professionals exploring a pivot into AI safety — non-technical and lightly-technical people from operations, journalism, policy, product, design, consulting, law, biotech, science communication, and similar fields. Also useful for software engineers and ML practitioners exploring generalist roles.

## What it covers

- Which AI safety role archetypes realistically fit your background
- What the bar looks like at specific orgs, not just "yes, ops matters"
- How to translate your existing skills into AI safety terms
- Whether you need to be technical (and how technical)
- Generalist, non-research, ops, comms, policy, hybrid, and founder paths
- Application advice specific to AI safety orgs
- When and how to route to a human advisor

## What it doesn't do

Course and fellowship recommendations for building competence — that's the [AI Safety Learning Paths](../ai-safety-learning-paths/) skill. Funding mechanics for a project or org you're founding — that's the [AI Safety Funding](../ai-safety-funding/) skill.
